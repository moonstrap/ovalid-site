How it works

Guided by numbers, not vibes

1

Live metric guidance

While you frame the shot, Ovalid measures head position, pose, lighting balance and background — and tells you exactly what to fix: “move closer”, “face the light source”, “find a plain background”. The oval turns green when everything checks out.

2

Scored against the real standard

On capture, your photo is assessed by OFIQ — the reference implementation of ISO/IEC 29794-5, the face image quality standard developed for EU border systems. You see actual scores per measure, not a thumbs-up.

3

Cropped to your document

Pick your country and document, and Ovalid crops to its exact geometry — head size, eye line, background rule — and exports print-ready and upload-ready files. Cropping is geometry, not alteration.

Why “never edited” matters

Most photo apps disqualify your photo

Popular ID-photo apps “fix” your picture with AI background replacement and skin smoothing. That’s exactly what passport authorities prohibit — the US State Department requires the original, unaltered photo and checks submissions for software manipulation.

Ovalid’s entire design is the opposite: help you take a photo that’s genuinely right, so nothing needs fixing. Measurement and cropping only. No retouching, no relighting, no beauty filter — and vendor camera “enhancement” is switched off at capture.
